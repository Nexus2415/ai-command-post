Resetting your password
  1. Open Vercel, project arnexyia-command-post.
  2. Settings → Environment Variables → ACP_SITE_PASSWORD → Edit, type a new password, Save.
  3. Deployments → the top one → ⋯ → Redeploy. Then sign in here with the new password.
Changing it signs out every device that was signed in.
